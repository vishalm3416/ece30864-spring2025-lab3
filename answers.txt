1. Pros and Cons of Agile Development:
   Pros:
   - Faster feedback and adaptability.
   - Increased collaboration and flexibility.
   Cons:
   - Requires constant communication, which can slow progress.
   - Difficult to predict timelines and scope creep is common.

2. Scrum Master vs Product Owner
Scrum Master Responsibilities:

Ensures the team follows Scrum principles and Agile best practices.
Facilitates daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives.
Removes obstacles that block team progress.
Acts as a coach for the development team to improve Agile efficiency.
Product Owner Responsibilities:

Defines the product vision and manages the backlog.
Prioritizes user stories based on business value and stakeholder input.
Communicates with stakeholders to refine requirements.
Ensures the development team delivers features that meet user needs.
