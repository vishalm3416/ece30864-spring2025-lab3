1. Pros and Cons of Agile Development:
   Pros:
   - Faster feedback and adaptability.
   - Increased collaboration and flexibility.
   Cons:
   - Requires constant communication, which can slow progress.
   - Difficult to predict timelines and scope creep is common.

2. Scrum Master vs Product Owner
Scrum Master Responsibilities:

Ensures the team follows Scrum principles and Agile best practices.
Facilitates daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives.
Removes obstacles that block team progress.
Acts as a coach for the development team to improve Agile efficiency.
Product Owner Responsibilities:

Defines the product vision and manages the backlog.
Prioritizes user stories based on business value and stakeholder input.
Communicates with stakeholders to refine requirements.
Ensures the development team delivers features that meet user needs.

3. Sprint Meetings and Their Discussions
Sprint Planning:

Define the sprint goal and scope.
Select user stories from the backlog.
Break stories into tasks and estimate effort.
Daily Stand-Up (Scrum Meeting):

What was completed yesterday?
What will be worked on today?
Any blockers or obstacles?
Sprint Review & Retrospective:

Sprint Review: Demonstrate completed work to stakeholders.
Sprint Retrospective: Discuss what went well, what didn’t, and what to improve.

4. JIRA Issue Types: Epic vs Story vs Bug vs Task
Epic:

A large feature or objective that consists of multiple stories.
Example: "Implement user authentication system."
Story:

A specific feature or requirement that delivers value.
Example: "As a user, I want to log in using Google OAuth."
Bug:

A defect or issue that needs to be fixed.
Example: "Login button crashes on Safari browser."
Task:

A general work item that needs to be completed.
Example: "Set up the database schema."
